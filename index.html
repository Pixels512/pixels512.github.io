Booklore — Book Tracker

A free, private reading tracker for iPhone. Track what you are reading, build your shelves, rate in half-stars, and see your reading year come together.

  • No account, and no server of ours ever holds your library
  • Works fully offline
  • Imports a Goodreads CSV export, and exports everything back out
  • Reading timer, calendar, streaks and yearly goal
